ORADENT — slider images
=======================

Drop a .jpg, .jpeg, .png or .webp file into THIS folder and it appears in the
slider on the home page and the Clinic page the next time the page loads.
Delete it and it disappears. Nothing else to change.

ORDER
  Files are sorted by filename, so the number prefix controls the sequence:
      01-treatment-room.jpg
      02-dr-nabanita-chaklader.jpg
      ...
  To move a photo, rename its prefix. New photos without a prefix sort last.

SIZE
  Photos straight off a phone are fine — the site resizes each one ONCE into
  the hidden .cache folder and serves that, so pages stay fast. Landscape
  photos (wider than tall) look best; the slide is 16:10.

WORDS
  The title on each slide is worked out from the filename, so name files in
  plain words: "07-waiting-area.jpg" becomes "Waiting area".
  For better wording and proper alt text, add an entry in captions.php.

DO NOT put patient photographs here unless that patient has given you
permission to publish their picture.
